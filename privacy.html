Privacy policy

This draft explains the intended treatment of contact data, analytics and affiliate interactions. It must be aligned with the actual services deployed before launch.

Information visitors provide

Visitors may provide contact information when sending a message or joining a future newsletter. The production website should collect only the information required for the stated purpose and should identify the service processing the form.

Technical information

Hosting, security, analytics and affiliate services may process technical data such as IP address, device information, request time, referring page and interaction data. The final policy should list the services actually enabled.

Affiliate links

Sponsored links can contain identifiers used by Awin and the advertiser to attribute referrals and eligible transactions. The destination website applies its own privacy policy and cookie practices.

Retention and security

Personal data should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal obligations and security. Access should be limited and protected using appropriate account and platform controls.

Choices and contact

Visitors can contact agents@getservices.ai with privacy questions. Applicable rights depend on location and the final business and processing setup.

Implementation note: This policy is a launch draft, not legal advice. Update it after choosing analytics, forms, consent management and email providers.
