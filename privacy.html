Privacy policy

This policy explains what information ticktes.online collects, how affiliate links and cookies work, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who we are

ticktes.online is an independent information and discovery website for tickets, passes, activities, tours and getaways. It is a GetServices.ai property. You can reach us at agents@getservices.ai or 313-729-1534.

Information we collect

Newsletter sign-ups

If you subscribe to our email newsletter, we collect your email address and your consent to receive emails. Sign-up forms on this site send this information to a form service operated for GetServices.ai, and some pages use the Web3Forms service to deliver it. We use it only to send you the newsletter, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

Messages you send us

If you email or call us, we keep your message and contact details so we can reply and handle corrections or partner inquiries. The contact page does not currently collect messages through an online form.

Technical information

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, device and browser details, the pages requested and the time of the request, for security and reliability. The search box on our pages runs in your browser and does not send what you type to us. We do not currently use analytics or advertising trackers of our own.

Cookies and affiliate links

ticktes.online does not set its own advertising or tracking cookies. Many links on this site are sponsored affiliate links, including links to GetOut through the Awin network and links to Tiqets. When you click one, the affiliate network or the seller may set a cookie or use a tracking identifier in the link so that a purchase can be credited to us. This costs you nothing extra. These companies handle your information under their own privacy policies, and the seller you buy from controls its own checkout, payment, cancellations and refunds. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; affiliate credit may then not be recorded. See our affiliate disclosure for more.

Third-party links

Our pages link to partner and other third-party websites. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies before sharing any information.

How we use and share information

We use the information above to send the newsletter you asked for, to reply to you, to keep the site secure and to credit affiliate referrals. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with others except the service providers described on this page, or where the law requires it. We keep newsletter details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and messages for as long as needed to deal with your request.

Your rights

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to ask us to delete it, to ask us to correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. Because we do not sell or share personal information for advertising, there is nothing to opt out of. Other visitors may have similar rights under their local laws. To use any of these rights, email agents@getservices.ai. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email agents@getservices.ai or call 313-729-1534.
