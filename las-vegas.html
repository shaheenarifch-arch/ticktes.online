Las Vegas attraction tickets & city passes

Shows, observation wheels, free Strip sights and day trips to the Hoover Dam and Grand Canyon: how to book Las Vegas tickets without overpaying.

Top Las Vegas attractions to book

These are the attractions most visitors plan a Las Vegas trip around, with what to check before you pay.

  • ShowsResidencies, magic, comedy and acrobatic shows. Seat location and day of the week change the price a lot.
  • High Roller observation wheelGiant observation wheel at The LINQ. Night rides cost more than daytime.
  • SphereImmersive venue with films and concerts. Check the current program and seating guidance.
  • Bellagio fountainsFree water and music show at regular intervals in the afternoon and evening.
  • Fremont Street ExperienceFree light canopy show downtown; the zip line costs extra.
  • Hoover Dam toursAbout 45 minutes from the Strip. Tour types and availability vary, so book ahead.
  • Grand Canyon day tripsThe West Rim is closest; the South Rim is a long day. Compare bus, plane and helicopter options carefully.
  • Red Rock CanyonScenic drive west of the city. A timed-entry reservation may be required in busy seasons.
Help: Hours, prices and booking rules change by season. Always confirm on the attraction's official site or the partner checkout page. Official information: Visit Las Vegas (official visitor guide) ↗ and Grand Canyon National Park (NPS) ↗.

Is a Las Vegas city pass worth it?

  • Count your paid stops. Passes usually break even at around three paid attractions. If your list is mostly free sights, skip the pass.
  • Check the clock. Many passes run for consecutive days from first use. Plan two or three attractions a day, not five.
  • Read reservation rules. Some included attractions still need a timed booking even with a pass.
  • Compare single tickets. For one or two big attractions, direct tickets are often cheaper.

Getting around and saving money

  • Walk or ride the Monorail. Many Strip resorts are connected by walkways; the Monorail runs behind the east side of the Strip. See Las Vegas Monorail routes ↗.
  • Book timed entry early for the most popular attractions, especially on weekends and holidays.
  • Mix free and paid. Pair a free sight in the morning with one paid attraction in the afternoon.
  • Arrive at opening time for the shortest lines. See how to skip lines at popular attractions.

Las Vegas tickets FAQ

How do I get cheaper Las Vegas show tickets?

Compare midweek dates and different seating sections, and buy from the venue or authorized sellers. Same-day discount booths can help for flexible plans.

Which Grand Canyon rim is best from Las Vegas?

The West Rim is the shortest trip. The South Rim is the classic national park view but is a much longer day.

What is free to do in Las Vegas?

The Bellagio fountains, Fremont Street light shows, the welcome sign and walking the Strip and resort lobbies are all free.
