Top Chicago attractions to book

These are the attractions most visitors plan a Chicago trip around, with what to check before you pay.

  • Skydeck ChicagoGlass ledges on the 103rd floor of Willis Tower. Lines can be long, so pick an off-peak time.
  • 360 ChicagoObservation deck in the former John Hancock Center, with the tilting TILT experience at extra cost.
  • Architecture river cruiseThe most popular way to see the city's buildings. Book a specific departure, especially in summer.
  • Art Institute of ChicagoMajor art museum next to Millennium Park. Check timed-entry and free-day rules for Illinois residents.
  • Shedd AquariumLakefront aquarium on the Museum Campus. Weekends sell out early.
  • Field MuseumNatural history museum, home of SUE the T. rex. Special exhibits cost extra.
  • Museum of Science and IndustryHuge hands-on museum in Hyde Park. Allow most of a day.
  • Millennium Park & Cloud GateFree. Combine with a walk along the lakefront and Maggie Daley Park.

Is a Chicago city pass worth it?

  • Count your paid stops. Passes usually break even at around three paid attractions. If your list is mostly free sights, skip the pass.
  • Check the clock. Many passes run for consecutive days from first use. Plan two or three attractions a day, not five.
  • Read reservation rules. Some included attractions still need a timed booking even with a pass.
  • Compare single tickets. For one or two big attractions, direct tickets are often cheaper.

Getting around and saving money

  • Use the L trains. The CTA L and buses reach most sights; the Museum Campus is walkable from downtown. Check current CTA fares ↗.
  • Book timed entry early for the most popular attractions, especially on weekends and holidays.
  • Mix free and paid. Pair a free sight in the morning with one paid attraction in the afternoon.
  • Arrive at opening time for the shortest lines. See how to skip lines at popular attractions.

Chicago tickets FAQ

Is a Chicago city pass worth it?

Usually only if you plan three or more paid attractions, such as an observation deck, the aquarium and a museum. Compare with single tickets first.

Skydeck or 360 Chicago?

Skydeck is higher and has the glass ledge; 360 Chicago is closer to the lake and Magnificent Mile. Most visitors choose one.

When is the architecture cruise season?

Most river cruises run spring through fall, with fewer departures in winter. Check the operator's schedule.
