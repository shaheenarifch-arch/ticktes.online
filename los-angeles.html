Top Los Angeles attractions to book

These are the attractions most visitors plan a Los Angeles trip around, with what to check before you pay.

  • Universal Studios HollywoodTheme park plus a working studio tour. Express passes cost more but save time on busy days.
  • Warner Bros. Studio TourGuided backlot and soundstage tour in Burbank. Tours run at set times.
  • Disneyland ResortTwo parks in Anaheim, about an hour south. Check date-based pricing on the official site.
  • Griffith ObservatoryFree admission and great city views; planetarium shows cost extra. Parking fills up fast.
  • The Getty CenterFree museum admission with a paid parking fee. Reservations may be suggested on busy days.
  • Hollywood Walk of FameFree. Combine with the TCL Chinese Theatre forecourt.
  • Santa Monica PierFree to visit; rides at the amusement park are paid.
  • Natural History Museum of LADinosaurs and gems in Exposition Park, near the California Science Center.
Help: Hours, prices and booking rules change by season. Always confirm on the attraction's official site or the partner checkout page. Official information: Discover Los Angeles (official visitor guide) ↗ and Griffith Observatory ↗.

Is a Los Angeles city pass worth it?

  • Count your paid stops. Passes usually break even at around three paid attractions. If your list is mostly free sights, skip the pass.
  • Check the clock. Many passes run for consecutive days from first use. Plan two or three attractions a day, not five.
  • Read reservation rules. Some included attractions still need a timed booking even with a pass.
  • Compare single tickets. For one or two big attractions, direct tickets are often cheaper.

Getting around and saving money

  • Plan around traffic. Group sights by area (Hollywood, Downtown, Santa Monica) and use Metro rail where it helps. Check current LA Metro fares ↗.
  • Book timed entry early for the most popular attractions, especially on weekends and holidays.
  • Mix free and paid. Pair a free sight in the morning with one paid attraction in the afternoon.
  • Arrive at opening time for the shortest lines. See how to skip lines at popular attractions.

Los Angeles tickets FAQ

Is a Los Angeles city pass worth it?

Only if your list has several paid attractions that are included. Many LA highlights, such as Griffith Observatory and the Getty, are free to enter.

Universal Studios or a studio tour?

Universal combines rides with a studio tram tour. A dedicated studio tour such as Warner Bros. is more behind-the-scenes and has no rides.

How do I avoid LA traffic?

Plan one area per day, avoid weekday rush hours, and use Metro rail for Downtown, Hollywood and Santa Monica where possible.
