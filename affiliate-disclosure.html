Affiliate disclosure

Some links on ticktes.online are sponsored affiliate links. This page explains that relationship in plain language.

How affiliate links work

When a visitor follows an identified sponsored link, a network or advertiser may record the referral. If the visitor later completes an eligible transaction under the campaign rules, ticktes.online may receive compensation.

Visitor price

Affiliate compensation does not create a separate ticktes.online fee. The partner controls the final price, taxes, payment terms and any service charges displayed during checkout.

Editorial independence

Compensation does not turn a partner offer into the right choice for every visitor. Editorial pages focus on selection criteria, restrictions and comparison questions. Visitors should evaluate the current partner information independently.

Awin and GetOut

The launch homepage and GetOut partner page include Awin tracking links and Awin-hosted creatives supplied for GetOut. These links are marked sponsored.

Changes to promotions

Affiliate promotions can be changed, paused or withdrawn by the network or advertiser. A creative shown on this website does not guarantee that the referenced product remains available.
