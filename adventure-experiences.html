How to choose adventure experiences

Adventure experiences can involve meaningful physical and environmental risk. Choose based on the operator’s published requirements and the participant’s health, confidence and recent experience.

Review whether equipment, instruction, protective clothing and transportation are included. If personal equipment is allowed, confirm applicable standards and inspection rules.

What to check before booking

  • Fitness and experience level
  • Age, height and weight limits
  • Medical disclosures
  • Included safety equipment
  • Qualified guide or instructor information
  • Weather and route changes
  • Insurance and waiver requirements
  • Emergency and cancellation procedures

Build a better comparison

Weather can change the route, schedule or feasibility of an activity. Understand who decides cancellation, what alternatives are offered and whether refunds or rescheduling apply.

Do not hide medical, mobility or experience information requested by the operator. Contact the provider before booking if a requirement is unclear or if a participant may need an adaptation.

FAQ

Adventure Experiences questions

What counts as an adventure experience?

The category can include guided outdoor, height, water, speed or skills-based activities. Each operator defines the exact activity and participation requirements.

Is equipment included?

Sometimes. Read the inclusion list for helmets, harnesses, wetsuits, footwear, transport or other required equipment.

What happens in bad weather?

The operator’s policy controls whether the activity proceeds, changes route, reschedules or cancels. Review the policy before travel.

Do participants need insurance?

Requirements differ by activity and destination. Check the operator terms and the traveler’s insurance coverage, exclusions and geographic limits.
